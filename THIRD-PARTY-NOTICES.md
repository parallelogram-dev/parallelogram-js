## Shopify Draggable

Sortable, Swappable and Droppable, and the drag engine behind them in `src/utils/draggable`, are a port of [Shopify Draggable](https://github.com/Shopify/draggable) 1.2. The engine, its Mirror, Scrollable and Focusable plugins, and the sortable, swappable and droppable behaviour come from it; the Pointer Events and keyboard sensors and the data attribute state are Parallelogram's own. Thank you to Shopify and Draggable's contributors.
